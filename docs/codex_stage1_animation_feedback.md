## Causes and leads (Claude's reading of the source; verify before relying on it)

- **Standing knife short (item 2).** In the solver (`player_anims.knife`, `measure.py`) the tip enters the skull at 0.27 s and sits mid-skull. In game it lands short, as it did before `PLAYER_SCALE`. The axe, bat and machete pairs, solved the same way, pass; the knife is the shortest reach and the tightest target, so small errors show. Candidates to check, in order: (a) the new sideways `react_knife` (walker_anims.reactions, kind "stab") throws the head toward the walker's right and back, so if the client plays the walker reaction even one or two ticks late against the player clip, the head has left the blade; compare the walker-head trajectory at 0.20-0.35 s with the old clip; (b) Player Animator body pivot is 0.7 blocks real (11.95 model px) against our 11.2 px, which with the knife's 14-17 deg body lean puts the hand ~0.25-0.5 px back; (c) slim (Alex) arms shift the hand 0.5 px (pa_item docstring); (d) the walker's GeckoLib render rotation `yBodyRot` lerp after `/hfa pair` re-places it. The reliable method is to measure in game: freeze the pair at 0.27-0.35 s (add a harness `/hfa freeze <t>` that sets Player Animator and GeckoLib speed to 0 at time t) and screenshot from the side and above.
- **Holds (item 3).** `player_weapons.hold()` solves one static weapon pose with `solve_weapon`/`solve_two` and adds only a 0.6 px "breath"; arms, torso and head barely move, and no lean is allowed (holds strip body/legs so they can layer over locomotion). Target poses were picked for readiness (machete raised by the shoulder `P(7, 24, -3)`, axe on the shoulder from `from_hands((-1, 16, -4), ...)`) rather than a relaxed carry. The axe's two-handed shoulder carry also puts a rigid vanilla arm through the chest; `body_penetration` is only a soft solver weight (`W_BODY`) and holds weren't audited.
- **Swings and locomotion (items 4, 5).** Hand-keyed with 3-5 keys and simple sine cycles; no anticipation, overlap, secondary motion or weight shift. Locomotion keys only legs, arms and a 2-9 deg lean.
- **Pistol shot sound (item 1).** TaCZ plays gun sounds from the gun pack's display data, per gun. Its jar has `com.tacz.guns.api.TimelessAPI.getGunDisplay(...)`, and the default pack under `server/tacz/tacz_default_gun/assets/tacz/` has the sound resources; inspect `GunDisplayInstance` (client) for the shoot sound ids with `javap` on `C:\HoldfastGate0\downloads\tacz-1.1.8-hotfix2\tacz-1.20.1-1.1.8-hotfix2.jar`. The server should trigger the sound at the execution's kill tick (it already knows the gun id via `TaczGuns`); TaCZ may also offer a server-side way to "fire" or play the shoot sound, which would keep muzzle flash and sound consistent. Don't use the presentation `fire` cue to decide anything.

## New direction from Andrew: replace vanilla animations

Andrew, 2026-10-17: "i want animations to start replacing the vanilla animations, so swings would be actual left click hits, walking would be the actual vanilla walk, same with sprinting and crouching, etc."

So the Holdfast clips stop being something you trigger with `/hfa` and become what the player actually does:
- **Left click** plays the held weapon family's swing (light; heavy when the attack bar is full or for a charged blunt release) instead of vanilla's arm swing, for melee weapons and empty hands (add a punch/shove family for empty hands).
- **Walking, sprinting, crouching, crouch-walking, jumping, idle** (and swimming/falling where it makes sense) play the Holdfast locomotion instead of vanilla's limb swing; injury states swap in the injury walks; a held TaCZ gun swaps in the legs-only `_gun` variants so TaCZ keeps arms, items and ADS.
- **Holding a melee weapon** plays its hold over the locomotion.
- Playback should follow real movement (scale the cycle by actual horizontal speed, blend in and out instead of popping, and play for every player each client sees, not only yourself).

Where it lives: `holdfast_core`'s `client/ClientClips` already has a first version of exactly this (locomotion 95 / weapon hold 97 / action 1100 layers, picked per player per tick from movement and the vanilla swing broadcast). It was built but **never seen in game** (Andrew only tested the `/hfa` harness). Make it the real system: drive it from actual input and state, add speed scaling and blending, cover empty hands and vanilla items (swords, axes) through the weapon-class mapping in `combat/Weapons`, and check it with and without TaCZ guns, in first and third person, and for remote players. The harness's `/hfa` stays as a test tool. This also raises the bar from items 3-5 below: the new holds, walks and swings are what players will see all the time, so they must look natural in continuous play, not just in isolation.
