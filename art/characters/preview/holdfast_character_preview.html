What this page is

  • A browser preview that plays the exported files. It is not Minecraft and nothing here is tested in game.
  • Textures are procedural placeholders. The player model is a stand-in for the vanilla player; weapons and crutches are preview props for item models.

The server decides

  • Grab start, the escape window, whether the key press landed in it, the bite region, and whether protection blocked it.
  • Execution requests, the commit point, and the brain kill. Animation events (bite, kill, shove) only time sounds and effects.

Variety without variants

  • Ordinary walkers mix 8 gaits, 6 arm sets and 6 head sets on three controllers. None changes speed class, senses or damage; they are not special variants.
